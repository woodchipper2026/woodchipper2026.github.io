OUR PLATFORM

A Clear Record. A Sharper Plan.

Three commitments. No fine print, no spin — just the work, done right.

PLANK ONE

Shred the Bureaucracy

Red tape doesn't serve anyone. The Wood Chipper will cut permitting delays, simplify local processes, and make government paperwork something you finish in an afternoon — not a season. Fewer forms, faster answers, cleaner outcomes.

PLANK TWO

Cut Through Waste

Every dollar of public money should work as hard as the people who earned it. That means open books, honest audits, and an end to spending that serves the process instead of the public. Efficiency isn't a slogan — it's the job.

PLANK THREE

Bring Results

Promises are cheap; results are what count. Safer streets, cleaner neighborhoods, and projects that actually finish. The Wood Chipper measures success the only way that matters: what got done, and who it helped.

COMMITTED TO EFFICIENCY ★ SAFETY ★ COMMUNITY
MEET THE CANDIDATE

Steady. Relentless. All Business.

The Wood Chipper has spent its entire career doing exactly what it says it will do. It doesn't grandstand, it doesn't flip-flop, and it has never once blamed someone else for a jam. When there's a pile of problems in front of it, it gets to work — and it doesn't stop until the job is done.

Supporters call it the hardest worker they'll never have to supervise. Critics call it single-minded. The record speaks for itself: everything that goes in comes out handled.

  • Position on the issues: unchanged since day one — it only sharpens them.
  • Work ethic: full days, no breaks, no excuses.
  • Special skill: turning big messy piles into something useful.
  • Campaign promise: a clean cut, every time.
